    What powers the analyzer

    From tokenization to probabilities, everything runs on your own backend.

    Deep Learning Powered

    A Bidirectional GRU reads the sentence forwards and backwards, so each word is understood in the context of the words around it.

    Natural Language Processing

    Text is lowercased and cleaned, then a Keras Tokenizer turns words into numbers the model can read.

    Six Emotion Detection

    Classifies text as sadness, joy, love, anger, fear or surprise.

    Real-Time Analysis

    Each prediction comes live from the connected FastAPI backend.

    Confidence Visualization

    See the probability the model gives to every emotion, not just the winner.

    Interactive Experience

    Try many sentences quickly, revisit past results and export a report.

    How it works

    Seven steps from your sentence to a prediction.

    1. 1

      You enter text

      Any sentence up to 2,000 characters.

    2. 2

      Preprocessing

      The text is lowercased and stripped of symbols.

    3. 3

      Tokenization

      The Keras Tokenizer maps words to numbers.

    4. 4

      Padding

      Sequences are fixed to 50 tokens.

    5. 5

      BiGRU model

      The network reads the sequence in both directions.

    6. 6

      Softmax

      Scores become probabilities across six emotions.

    7. 7

      Result

      The top emotion and its confidence appear here.
